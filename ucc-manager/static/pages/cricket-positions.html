Fielding Positions

Fielding positions for a right-handed batsman. Off side = to the batsman's left when facing the bowler; Leg side = to the right.

Field Diagram
Wicket-keeper
Stands directly behind the stumps. Catches edges, effects stumpings and run-outs. The only fielder who can wear gloves.
Off Side (behind square)
1st–3rd SlipLine of catchers behind the batsman on the off side. Key for edge catches off fast bowlers.
GullySquarish position between slip and point, catches square cuts.
Third ManDeep behind square on off side, cuts off fine edges to the boundary.
Off Side (in front of square)
PointSquare on the off side, stops cut shots.
Cover / Extra CoverBetween point and mid-off, covers drives through the off side.
Mid-offStraight on the off side near the bowler, stops straight drives.
Long-offDeep version of mid-off, cuts off lofted drives.
Leg Side
Fine LegBehind square on leg side, stops fine glances and edges.
Square LegSquare on leg side, stops pulls and sweeps.
Mid-wicketBetween square leg and mid-on, stops on-drives.
Mid-onStraight on the leg side near the bowler, stops on-drives.
Long-onDeep version of mid-on, cuts off lofted on-drives.
Deep sq. legDeep square leg, saves boundaries from pulls and sweeps.
