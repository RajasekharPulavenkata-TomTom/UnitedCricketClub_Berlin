1. General Conduct
  • Respect
    All members must show respect towards teammates, opponents, and any officials or ground staff. Abusive language or aggressive behavior won't be tolerated.
  • Sportsmanship
    Play in the spirit of the game. Shake hands before and after matches, and acknowledge good play from both sides.
  • Safety First
    Always prioritize safety. Be aware of your surroundings, especially when batting or bowling, and ensure equipment is used responsibly.
  • Punctuality
    Arrive on time for matches and practice sessions. If you're going to be late or absent, inform the group organizers as soon as possible.
  • Equipment Care
    Treat all shared equipment (if any) with care and report any damage.
2. Group Organization & Communication
  • Communication Channel
    All official group communication, match schedules, and updates will be shared in the United Cricket Club (UCC) WhatsApp group. Members are expected to check this group regularly.
  • Availability
    Members must update the dedicated availability sheet at least three days before any scheduled match. This helps organizers plan teams effectively.
  • Decision Making
    All decisions regarding rules, scheduling, or finances will be discussed and agreed upon by the majority of active group members.
  • Club Management & Captaincy
    The role of club management and captaincy will be separate. The captain will be selected through a team decision, not by any individual. Furthermore, management will not be involved in team selection for matches.
  • Player Opportunity & Communication
    If a player is not getting an opportunity to play in an upcoming match, the captain will provide a proper explanation to that player before the match.
  • Rule Adherence & Concerns
    Every person in the group has the right to raise a concern when a rule is broken.
  • Confidentiality
    Please refrain from sharing internal club information with members of other clubs or external parties before discussing it internally within the UCC WhatsApp group or with club management.
  • Feedback & Satisfaction
    Every member has the right to raise a concern if they are not enjoying the game, or in cases of miscommunication, misconduct, or general dissatisfaction. Your feedback is important for the group's harmony and enjoyment.
