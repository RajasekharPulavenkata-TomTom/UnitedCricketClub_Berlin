Cricket Glossary

Key cricket terms explained.

Batting
Duck
—
A score of 0 runs. A "golden duck" is dismissed on the first ball.
Century / Ton
—
100 or more runs in a single innings.
Half-century / Fifty
—
50 or more runs in a single innings.
Strike Rate
—
Runs scored per 100 balls faced. Higher = more aggressive batting.
Batting Average
—
Total runs ÷ number of dismissals. Measures consistency.
Crease
—
The lines in front of and around the stumps. Batters must ground bat or body behind it to be safe.
Pull Shot
—
A horizontal bat shot played to a short delivery on the leg side.
Sweep
—
A low, horizontal bat shot played to spin bowling on the leg side.
Drive
—
An upright, full-bat shot played to fuller-length deliveries. Off drive, on drive, straight drive, cover drive.
Reverse Sweep
—
A sweep shot played in the opposite direction, catching fielders off guard.
Bowling
Economy Rate
—
Runs conceded per over. Lower = more economical bowling.
Bowling Average
—
Runs conceded per wicket taken. Lower = more effective bowling.
Hat-trick
—
Taking three wickets on three consecutive deliveries.
Maiden Over
—
An over in which no runs are scored (excluding extras).
Yorker
—
A delivery aimed at the batsman's feet / base of the stumps. Very difficult to hit.
Bouncer
—
A short-pitched delivery that rears up towards the batsman's head or chest.
Full Toss
—
A delivery that reaches the batsman without bouncing.
Swing
—
Lateral movement of the ball in the air. Inswing moves into the batsman; outswing moves away.
Seam
—
Movement off the pitch using the ball's seam. Can deviate either way after bouncing.
Off-spin
—
Spin bowling that turns from off to leg (for a right-hander facing a right-arm off-spinner).
Leg-spin
—
Spin bowling that turns from leg to off — typically harder to bowl but more dangerous.
Googly
—
A leg-spinner's delivery that turns the opposite way (off to leg) as a disguised surprise.
Doosra
—
An off-spinner's delivery that turns the opposite way, similar to a googly for off-spin.
Match Terms
Innings
—
The batting turn of a team or individual. Each team has one innings per limited-overs game; two in Tests.
Over
—
6 legal deliveries from one end. The bowler must change after each over.
Run Rate
—
Runs scored per over. Used to compare team performance and calculate D/L targets.
Powerplay
—
Fielding restrictions phase — only 2 (T20) or 4 (ODI) fielders outside the 30-yard circle.
D/L Method
—
Duckworth-Lewis-Stern method. Recalculates target scores when a match is interrupted by rain.
Super Over
—
A tie-breaking single over per team played after a tied limited-overs match.
Toss
—
A coin flip before the match. The winning captain chooses to bat or field first.
Follow-on
—
In Tests, forcing the opposition to bat again immediately if they trail by 200+ runs.
Declaration
—
When the batting captain voluntarily closes their innings in Test cricket.
LBW
—
Leg Before Wicket. Out if the ball would have hit the stumps but struck the leg instead of the bat.
Review (DRS)
—
A team's challenge to an umpire's decision using technology. Each team gets 2 per innings.
