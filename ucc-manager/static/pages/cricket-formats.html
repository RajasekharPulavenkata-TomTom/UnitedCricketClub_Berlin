Cricket Formats

Cricket is played in different formats, each with its own rules, strategy, and flavour.

T10
Ten10 — Fastest Format
10
Overs / side
~90 min
Duration
  • 2-over Powerplay — only 2 fielders outside the 30-yard circle.
  • Max 2 overs per bowler.
  • Free hit after every no-ball.
  • Super Over to break ties.
  • Biggest league: Abu Dhabi T10 League.
Strategy: Every ball counts — batters attack from ball one. Bowlers must be accurate; there is no time to settle.
T20
Twenty20
20
Overs / side
~3 hrs
Duration
  • 6-over Powerplay — only 2 fielders allowed outside the 30-yard circle.
  • Max 4 overs per bowler.
  • Free hit after every no-ball.
  • Super Over to break ties.
  • Highest-scoring, most explosive format.
  • Biggest leagues: IPL, Big Bash, The Hundred.
UCC Format: Most local club cricket, including UCC matches, is played in T20 or similar short formats.
